# Nudge

## The problem
You have your calendar alerts turned on, and you still miss meetings. The little banner slides into the corner of your screen while you're deep in something else, and it's gone before you notice. By the time you look up, the call started ten minutes ago.

## How it helps
- Five minutes before a meeting, Nudge plays a sound and shows a banner, so you have time to wrap up.
- One minute before, it fills your whole screen with the meeting's name and a countdown. You can't miss it.
- If the meeting has a video link, one click on Join takes you straight into the call.
- It sits quietly in your menu bar and reads the Google Calendar you've already added to your Mac, so you can see what's on now and next at a glance.

## Who it's for
- People on a Mac who live in Google Calendar and keep getting pulled into focused work.
- Anyone who has ever said "sorry, I didn't see the reminder" at the start of a call.
