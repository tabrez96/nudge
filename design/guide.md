# Nudge: guide

## Getting started
1. Make sure your Google Calendar is on your Mac: open System Settings, go to Internet Accounts, add your Google account and turn on Calendars. Nudge needs macOS 13 or newer.
2. Download the newest Nudge from its Releases page on GitHub, open the download and drag Nudge onto Applications. The first time you open it, your Mac may block it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
3. Open Nudge. A bell appears in your menu bar (Nudge has no window and no Dock icon). When your Mac asks whether Nudge may send notifications, click Allow.
4. Click the bell. If it says "Nudge can't see your calendar yet", choose Allow calendar access… and say yes. If your Google account isn't on this Mac yet, choose Add Google account in Internet Accounts… instead. (S01)
5. Once the menu says "Synced just now", open the Calendar submenu and tick the calendar you want Nudge to watch. (S01)
6. Choose Test the alerts to see and hear what's coming: a banner with a sound, then the full-screen warning. Click Got it to close it. (S01, S03, S02)

Nudge only warns you while it's running, so leave it open. If you quit it, open it again from Applications.

## How to use it
**Get ready when the five-minute banner arrives**
1. Five minutes before a meeting, you hear a sound and a banner shows the meeting's name, time, place and the start of its notes. (S03)
2. Use the time to wrap up. If the meeting has a video link, click Join on the banner to go straight in. (S03)

**Get into the call at the one-minute warning**
1. One minute before the start, your whole screen turns red with the meeting's name and a countdown. (S02)
2. Click Join to open the call; the warning closes on every screen. (S02)
3. Or click Got it, or press Return or Esc, to close it without joining. Return never joins a call by accident. (S02)

**See what's on now and next, and fix problems**
1. Click the bell in the menu bar. A meeting that's running shows under Now, and your next meetings today show under Next meetings. (S01)
2. Click a meeting with a Join label to open its call. (S01)
3. If the bell has a red dot, open the menu to see what's wrong. Nudge keeps warning you from the meetings it last saw. Choose Retry now once you're back online. (S01)
